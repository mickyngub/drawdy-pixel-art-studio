# Pixel Art Studio (⌐■_■)

**retro sprites, right on your board.**

A pixel grid with PICO-8, Game Boy and NES colours that drops your art on the board as a crisp sprite, sharp at any zoom. Sketch a game jam hero, a sticker for your notes or a tiny avatar for every teammate ✧

![Opening Pixel Art Studio, painting a small sprite with the pencil and fill, switching to the Game Boy palette, placing it on the board and zooming in to show its crisp pixels.](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mickyngub/drawdy-pixel-art-studio/main/docs/media/pixel-art-studio.gif)

---

## let's paint ٩(◕‿◕)۶

1. Click the **pixel heart** in the extension rail.
2. Click a colour in the swatches and paint with **Pencil**, **Fill** or **Line**. **Pick** grabs a colour from the grid and **Eraser** tidies up.
3. Click **Place on board**. Your sprite lands in view, next to anything already there, with a transparent background.
4. To change it later, select it, click **Edit selected**, paint, then click **Update**.

> (・_・;) **⌘Z undid my pixels, not the board?** In the panel, ⌘Z and Ctrl+Z undo the grid; click the board first to undo there.

---

## make it yours (｡•̀ᴗ-)✧

- **Palette**: PICO-8, Game Boy, NES or Custom. Switching moves every pixel to the nearest colour, and ⌘Z brings the old ones back.
- **Size**: 16 × 16 up to 64 × 64, keeping the top-left corner in place.
- **Mirror**: Pencil, Eraser and Line paint both halves at once, for faces and spaceships.
- **Colour**: with Custom, set any of the 16 swatches to the colour you want.
- **Clear**: start over in one click, and ⌘Z if you didn't mean it.

## little things to try (っ˘ω˘ς )

- A 16 × 16 hero and a slime on a game jam pitch board.
